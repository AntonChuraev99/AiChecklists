How to connect Gisti to Claude

Connect once per Claude app. It takes about a minute.

info

First, sign in to Gisti.

Open the Gisti app or web app and sign in with Google at least once, so your account and checklists exist.

open_in_new Open Gisti
Your Gisti MCP server URL
https://gisti-mcp.gisti.workers.dev/mcp

arrow_downward Use this same URL in every Claude app below.

desktop_windows

Claude Desktop & claude.ai

  1. Open Settings → Connectors (on claude.ai: profile → Settings → Connectors).
  2. Click Add custom connector.
  3. Paste the server URL above.
  4. Click Add, then Connect.
  5. Sign in with Google and authorize access to your checklists.
terminal

Claude Code

  1. Add the server: 
    claude mcp add --transport http gisti https://gisti-mcp.gisti.workers.dev/mcp
  2. Run /mcp, choose gisti, follow the prompt to authenticate.
  3. Sign in with Google in the browser and authorize.

lock Claude connects from Anthropic's cloud, so this keeps working even when the Gisti app is closed.

Gisti MCP: questions, answered

Is Gisti MCP free? expand_more

Yes. Connecting Gisti to Claude is free and included with your Gisti account. The AI tools (create and fill) use your daily AI credits — 10/day on Free, 300/day on Premium — just like in the app; everything else is free.

Which Claude apps does it work with? expand_more

Any Claude client that supports custom connectors or remote MCP: Claude Desktop, claude.ai in the browser, and Claude Code. You connect once per app with the same server URL. (On Claude's Free plan you can add one custom connector.)

Is my data safe? What can Claude access? expand_more

You connect with Google, and Claude can only reach your own checklists — never anyone else's. Nothing is shared until you connect, and you decide what to ask. Remove the connector anytime to cut off access.

Does it work while the Gisti app is closed? expand_more

Yes. Gisti MCP is a remote server, so Claude talks to your account directly — your phone or browser doesn't need to be open. Your changes appear on every device the next time you open Gisti.

What if I'm not signed in to Gisti? expand_more

Open Gisti and sign in with Google at least once so your account exists. If you connect before signing in, Claude won't find any checklists to work with.

How do I disconnect Gisti from Claude? expand_more

Remove the Gisti connector in your Claude settings — or in Claude Code run claude mcp remove gisti. Access ends immediately, and your checklists stay safe in your account.
